Composition of two rank-1 MultiDiagonal operators: \((A|B)_{nm} = \sum_k A_{nk} B_{km}\).

Result diagonals appear at all pairwise offset sums; see MultiDiagonal.cc for the four upper/lower cases.

Note
Only rank-1 composition is currently implemented.
operator* has higher precedence than operator| in C++; use parentheses in mixed expressions.
